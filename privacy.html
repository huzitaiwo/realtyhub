About this notice

REALTY is a product concept interface by Handsome. It shows how a property marketplace could work: searching, comparing, saving, viewings, mortgage estimates and tools for agents. Property availability and financial figures shown are representative demos.

This notice explains, in plain language, what the current version does with information you enter. It describes how the site behaves today, and it will be updated before any real accounts, payments or messaging are added.

Information you enter

You may type information into the site as you use it. Depending on what you use, this can include:

  • Your name, email address, phone number and home preferences, in Profile.
  • Saved homes, saved searches and homes selected for comparison.
  • Viewing requests, including the date, time and contact details you enter.
  • Messages and enquiries you write to an agency.
  • Listing details, photos and team details, if you use the agent or agency tools.
  • Questions you send through the Help centre form.

Please do not enter information you would not want stored in your own browser.

How it is used

Information you enter is used only to make the features you use work, for example to show your saved homes again or to fill in a form with your name. In this version of the site it is not sent to a server, sold, or used for advertising.

What is stored on your device

REALTY does not set tracking cookies. It uses your browser's local storage to remember what you enter. This data stays in your browser on this device.

Items kept in local storage
Item What it remembers
realty:saved Homes you have saved
realty:compare Homes selected for comparison
realty:searches Your saved searches and alert settings
realty:viewings Viewing requests and their status
realty:messages Conversations with agencies
realty:notifications Notifications and whether you have read them
realty:profile Your profile and home preferences
realty:settings Your alert, display and privacy settings
realty:support Messages sent through the Help centre form
realty:custom-listings, realty:leads Listings and leads in the agent tools
realty:agency, realty:team, realty:assignments Agency profile, team members and listing assignments

Third-party services

The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. When a page loads, your browser requests the font files from Google, which can receive technical information such as your IP address. The simulated map and the virtual tour are built into the site and do not contact a map or tour provider.

A later version may use Firebase for accounts and storage. This notice will be updated before that happens.

Your choices

  • Use Clear data in Settings to remove everything REALTY has saved in this browser.
  • Clear your browser's site data for the same result.
  • Choose whether to share your profile with agents in Settings.
  • Pause or delete a saved search, or cancel a viewing, whenever you like.

Children

REALTY is intended for adults looking to buy, rent or list a home. It is not directed at children.

Changes

If how the site handles information changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change.

Contact

Questions about this notice can be sent through the Help centre.
